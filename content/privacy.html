Privacy

Your details, and what we do with them.

In plain words. Last updated 23 November 2026.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the enquiry form, by email or on WhatsApp: your name, email address, and, if you give them, your phone number, your organisation, your budget range, your timing and what you tell us about your project.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to make your film. We do not sell your details, add you to a mailing list or share them with anyone for their own use.

Who handles it for us

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes enquiries from the form to our inbox and sends you a copy.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our email.
  • WhatsApp carries any messages you send us there.
  • Netlify hosts this website and, like any web host, keeps short-lived technical logs of visits.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site itself.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to reply to you and to keep our business records. Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
