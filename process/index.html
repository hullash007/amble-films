Our process

A clear path from brief to final film.

Four stages, with your input and approval built into each one, and a free AI video preview for qualified projects before you commit.

  1. 01

    Brief

    One conversation about what the film must achieve, who it is for and where it will run.

    Your input
    Your goals, audience, brand guidelines and any references.
    You receive
    A written brief we both agree on.
  2. 02

    Preview

    We develop the concept, treatment and look. For qualified projects, we create a complimentary AI video preview of 30 to 45 seconds, so you can judge the direction on screen. Then we agree the full scope, timeline and price.

    Your input
    Your notes on the preview and the direction.
    You receive
    A preview to react to, then the plan and a fixed price, approved before production.
  3. 03

    Production

    We generate the scenes with AI, direct and select every shot, edit the film and share cuts as it takes shape.

    Your input
    Timed notes on each cut, within the agreed rounds.
    You receive
    Cuts to review, and changes made from your notes.
  4. 04

    Delivery

    Sound design, music, voice and colour grade, then final masters in every format you need.

    Your input
    Final approval.
    You receive
    Masters, cut-downs and captions, ready to publish.

Questions

What clients ask before they start

Do you really use AI to make the videos?

Yes. We use generative AI to create the scenes, characters, settings and visual effects in our films. Our team writes, directs, selects, edits, scores and grades every film, so the result is intentional and on brand.

Will the film look obviously made by AI?

We direct every shot and discard most of what the tools generate, then edit, grade and mix the film by hand. Watch the films on this site and judge for yourself.

What is the free AI video preview?

For qualified projects, we create a complimentary AI video preview of 30 to 45 seconds based on your brief, in one creative direction. If it isn't right for you, there is nothing to pay and no obligation. If you want to go ahead, we agree the full scope, timeline and price before full production begins. Previews are for review only and cannot be published or used commercially unless the project is commissioned.

How much does a film cost?

Selected AI video projects start from $100. Every quote depends on the brief, complexity, length, number of deliverables and how the film will be used, and you receive a fixed price before production starts.

Do you work with enterprise teams and agencies?

Yes. We work to your brand guidelines, agreed deliverables, review rounds and approval stages, and can produce a continuing programme of videos. Agencies can brief us as a production partner on their own scripts and boards.

Can the same characters appear across a series?

Yes. We keep characters, settings and style consistent from film to film, so a series feels like one piece of work.

How do you make sure the content is accurate?

We work from your material. You approve the script and storyboard before anything is generated, and you review cuts before we finish.

How long does it take?

That depends on the length and the number of films. Your timeline comes with the fixed quote, before production begins.

Where can we use the finished film?

Tell us where it will run, whether that is your website, YouTube, paid ads, a classroom or an event, and the usage is written into your quote.

Do you work with clients internationally?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide. Briefs, reviews and approvals happen over calls and shared review links, arranged around your time zone.

Have a story, a product or an idea that deserves a film?

Tell us what it needs to do. We will reply with questions, a direction and a fixed quote, and for qualified projects, a free AI video preview.
