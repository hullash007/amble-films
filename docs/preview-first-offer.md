# Complimentary AI video preview: internal policy

Internal only. `/docs` returns 404 on the website. Approved by the owner on 25 November 2026 (preview length: 30 to 45 seconds).

## What the website promises (public wording, in `content/site.mjs` → `PREVIEW`)

For qualified projects, we create a complimentary AI video preview of 30 to 45 seconds, built around the brief.
If it isn't right for the client, they owe nothing for it and there is no obligation to continue.
If they love the direction, we agree the full scope, timeline and price before full production begins.
One complimentary preview per qualified project. Previews are for review only and are not licensed for publication
or commercial use unless the project is commissioned.

## Who qualifies (decided by Amble Films, case by case)

A business, brand, agency or organisation with a genuine project. The enquiry should give, or a short reply should establish:

- the business or brand
- what they want to create
- the intended audience
- where the film will be used
- a brief, script or reference
- an expected timeline

Amble Films may decline a preview request without giving a reason. Not every enquiry qualifies, and the website never says it does.

## What the preview is

- One preview per qualified project (and in practice one per organisation at a time).
- 30 to 45 seconds, in one creative direction.
- One round of written feedback on the preview. No further free revisions.
- Watermarked. Remains the property of Amble Films. No usage rights unless the project is commissioned.
- Not a finished campaign and not a set of variations.

## After the preview

- Not right for the client: nothing is owed, and there is no obligation.
- Client wants to proceed: scope, deliverables, timeline, usage and price are agreed in writing before full production. The preview direction may be developed into the final film.

## Not offered (never publish)

Unlimited free videos, unlimited revisions, automatic eligibility, refunds or money-back guarantees, or any legal promise
beyond the wording above.
