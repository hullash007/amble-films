## Recommendation: both models, chosen by project size

| | Model A: Direction Preview | Model B: Paid Pilot |
|---|---|---|
| For | Qualified enquiries for a single film or a short series | Larger or recurring work: series, campaigns, monthly production |
| What the client sees | A creative direction: one-page treatment, 6 to 10 styled frames, and a short **rough** motion test of about 10 to 20 seconds, watermarked, low resolution, no final sound | A short **finished** sequence (about 20 to 45 seconds) at final quality, so they judge real production standard |
| Price | Free to qualified clients (your cost is the investment) | Paid, fixed fee; credited against the full production if they proceed within an agreed window |
| Directions | One direction (two only if the brief is genuinely open, at your discretion) | One agreed direction |
| Revisions | None on the preview itself; feedback goes into the production quote | One round of notes |
| Rights | No usage rights. Watermarked, streamed via review link, not downloadable | Usage rights only as written in the pilot agreement, after payment |

**Why both:** A builds confidence at low cost and protects the "see before you commit" promise. B stops the free
offer being used to extract real production work on large projects, and turns proof of quality into revenue.

## Expected effort (estimates; replace with your own time logs after the first five)

| Item | Model A | Model B |
|---|---|---|
| Brief call and qualification | 0.5 to 1 hour | 1 hour |
| Treatment and styled frames | 3 to 5 hours | 4 to 6 hours |
| Motion test / pilot production | 3 to 6 hours (rough) | 10 to 20 hours (finished, with edit, sound and grade) |
| Tool and generation costs | Low; set a hard cap per preview | Included in the pilot fee |
| **Total** | **about 7 to 12 hours** | **about 15 to 27 hours** |

Rule of thumb: cap free previews at a number you can afford each month (for example three), and track conversion.
If fewer than roughly one in three previews converts to paid work, tighten eligibility before reducing quality.

## Commercial safeguards (proposed terms)

1. **Eligibility (A):** a complete brief, a named decision-maker, a stated budget at or above a minimum you set, and a
   realistic timeline. Not offered for speculative pitches, competitions, or requests for "samples" only.
2. **Scope cap (A):** one direction, about 10 to 20 seconds of rough motion, delivered within an agreed number of working days.
3. **Watermark and resolution:** every preview watermarked "Amble Films preview, not for use", maximum 720p, shared by
   streaming review link, no download, no source files.
4. **Intellectual property:** the concept, treatment, frames and footage remain Amble Films property. No licence to use,
   adapt or have another studio reproduce them unless the client commissions the production.
5. **Pilot fee (B):** fixed and paid in advance; credited in full against the production fee if the client proceeds
   within, for example, 60 days; not refundable otherwise.
6. **Production milestones (after either):** for example 50% on approval of the direction and quote, 50% before
   delivery of clean masters. Final, unwatermarked, full-resolution files and usage rights are released only after full payment.
7. **Revisions in production:** a stated number of rounds (for example two) at each review stage; extra rounds quoted.
8. **Cancellation:** work completed up to the cancellation date is payable; deposits cover work done.
9. **No guarantees:** no promise of results, views or sales; no refund of production fees for subjective dislike after
   the direction was approved.

## Advantages

- Lowers the biggest barrier for first-time clients: uncertainty about how an AI-assisted film will look.
- Shows creative direction, which is what clients are actually buying, before asking for money.
- The qualification step filters out low-intent enquiries before they take your time.
- Pilot credit (B) makes the larger commitment feel safe without giving away production.

## Risks and how the terms handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Free-work seekers and concept harvesting | Eligibility rules, watermark, no download, IP clause |
| Time drain if conversion is low | Monthly cap on free previews; track conversion |
| Client expects the preview to be the final quality | Call it a *rough direction*; Model B exists for quality proof |
| Scope creep ("just one more version") | One direction, no revisions on the preview |
| Unclear rights disputes | Written preview terms accepted before work starts |
